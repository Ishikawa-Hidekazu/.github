# Code Of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free open source community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, behavior, documentation, and evidence.
- Be patient with people who are new to the project.
- Respect privacy and the documented safety boundaries of each project.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Posting credentials, tokens, cookies, private content, raw transcripts, or customer data.
- Deliberately bypassing a project's safety boundary.
- Repeated disruptive or deceptive behavior.

## Scope And Enforcement

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other public project communication.

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, lock, or restrict participation that violates this policy. Report conduct concerns privately through [ishikawa.co/contact](https://ishikawa.co/contact/) and include only the information needed to evaluate the report.
